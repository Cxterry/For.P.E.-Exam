文中已经给出要求:Dear,...
文中没给出:Dear Sir or Madam,
正文:
第一段：自我介绍+写作目的
I am+文中给出的线索.(1)The reason why I am writing the letter,to be honest,is to____

自我介绍如果发现不了明显提示线索：I am a senior  from the Department of Electronic Information Engineering in this/a university.

This letter, to be honest,seeks to___
